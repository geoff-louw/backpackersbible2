Budget Accommodation In Navarre

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

ALBERGUE PUENTE

STREET ADDRESS: P.º los Fueros, 57, 31100 Puente la Reina, Navarra

PHONE: +34 661 70 56 42

WEBSITE: alberguepuente.com

EMAIL: albergue@alberguepuente.com

GOOGLE RATING: 4.1 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com

Albergue Puente is a pilgrim albergue in the old town of Puente la Reina, at Paseo de los Fueros 57, in a former hostal built against the medieval wall. It has 40 places in shared mixed dorms with bunks and in double rooms with separate beds, six showers in shared bathrooms with hot water around the clock, and a power point and light at every bed. Guests can use a free, fully equipped kitchen with free coffee and herbal tea at any hour, a large dining room, a lounge, a terrace looking over the Valdizarbe valley, a washer and dryer for a small fee, a drying line and free Wi-Fi. Bed linen and towels can be rented, and there is covered bike parking. The medieval bridge that starts stage six is 50 metres away and the bus stop is opposite. Reception is open from 12:30 to 18:00, with automatic check-in on request, lights go out at 20:00 and check-out is by 8:30. Licence UAB00087.

Albergue San Nicolás is a newly built pilgrim albergue and rural guesthouse at Calle Sorandi 5-7 in Larrasoaña, a small Pyrenean village on the Camino de Santiago close to Pamplona. Its website describes it as modern, impeccable and welcoming, with large rooms that hold only a few beds each, eight full bathrooms next to the rooms and an equipped kitchen. It takes pilgrims, small groups, families and walkers, and offers home-cooked dinners based on Navarran produce. The village is surrounded by beech, oak and fir woods and rivers, and the owners can help plan trips to places such as the Oteiza Museum, the Irati forest and the Quinto Real valley, and for cycling, hiking and birdwatching. Booking is online through its site, by phone or by WhatsApp. The albergue is registered with Navarre tourism under number UAB00095 and can be contacted by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Booking.com

Aloha Hostel is on the first floor of the building at Calle Sangüesa 2 in the centre of Pamplona, where the Old Town ends and the Ensanche begins. It has four dormitories for about 25 to 26 people, plus private rooms with shared or private bathrooms, women-only rooms for up to three, and apartments. Guests get free Wi-Fi, a self-service breakfast from 05:00 to 10:00 that the site describes as basic, a shared equipped kitchen, free luggage storage and a terrace. Laundry and towels on the bunks cost extra. Check-in is from 13:00 to 22:00, with a door code for later arrivals, and check-out is by 11:00. It works as a private pilgrim albergue on the Camino Francés, with backpack pick-up from transport agencies, Pamplona's official stamp, closed bike storage on the terrace and a socket at each bunk. The bus station is 200 metres away. Pets are not allowed and there is no private parking.

Booking.com

Casa de la Abuela is a private pilgrim albergue in a traditional nineteenth-century house at Plaza de la Fruta 8 in the centre of Los Arcos, restored in 2010 to host walkers on the Camino Francés. The owners say the family has welcomed visitors there since 1955, and the albergue promotes a homely, shared atmosphere: coffee is prepared each morning, every evening there is a communal dinner where pilgrims sit at one table, and laundry is done communally and ecologically to save water and energy. It has two double rooms, a four-bed bunk room and a twelve-bed bunk room, with breakfasts, dinners and a bed-linen set available as extras. Check-in is from 13:00 to 18:00 and check-out is before 08:00. The albergue is registered as UAB00051, and its site credits funding from the Navarre employment service and the EU's NextGenerationEU programme.

Booking.com

Mesón del Barro is a family-run hostal with apartments at Calle Acella 2-bis in Pamplona, entered from Avenida de Navarra opposite the side of the Clínica Universitaria and behind Yamaguchi Park. Both the hostal and the apartments were fully renovated in 2022. The hostal rents single, double, triple and quadruple rooms, each with either a shared or a private bathroom, and breakfast can be added when you book. The location, near the University of Navarra and the city's main hospitals, is about a 15-minute walk from the old town and a five-minute drive from the Landaben industrial estate, so it suits visitors with business or medical appointments as well as travellers. Reception is open every day from 09:30 to 23:30, and from 13:30 on Sundays and public holidays. The owners offer special rates for direct bookings, weekly or monthly stays and groups. It is registered as UHS00742.

Booking.com

Albergue Plaza Catedral is a private albergue at Calle Navarrería 35, facing Pamplona Cathedral in the heart of the Old Town, close to the Navarrería square and the city walls. It occupies a protected historic building that has been given a modern, functional interior. From Holy Week to 1 November it focuses on pilgrims of the Camino de Santiago, except during San Fermín, and the rest of the year it takes tourists, travellers and groups, though it does not accept stag parties. Rooms are named after Navarre's mountains: Adi (10 beds in bunks, adapted for reduced mobility), Aralar (14 beds), the small closed Bi Aizpe (4 beds), the triple Ipasate with cathedral views and the compartmentalised gallery Piskerra. Every bed has its own socket and a key locker, and guests share an equipped kitchen, a dining room and a lounge with cable TV. Entry is by personal door code, and its site links to Albergue Puente in Puente la Reina as the next stage.

RESIDENCIA DE ESTUDIANTES STEPHOUSE PAMPLONA MONJARDÍN

STREET ADDRESS: C. Aoiz, 15, Bajo, 31004 Pamplona, Navarra

PHONE: +34 848 88 00 26

WEBSITE: stephouse.es

EMAIL: hello@stephouse.es

WHATSAPP: +34 608 93 54 39

GOOGLE RATING: 4.5 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com

StepHouse Pamplona Monjardín is a student residence, not a hostel, in a central, lively neighbourhood of Pamplona, about ten minutes from the centre and roughly 250 metres from the University of Navarra, with the public university about 20 minutes away by public transport. The railway station is around ten minutes from the residence. Rooms are studios with a private bathroom and kitchen, or private rooms in shared apartments for two or three people, with a study area, smart TV and climate control. Shared spaces include a social room, a terrace, a gym, coworking space and bookable private rooms with kitchens, and rent covers water, electricity and internet. There is a laundry managed through an app, 24-hour CCTV and card access. StepHouse mainly takes students for the academic year but also offers short stays of 1 to 30 days, aimed at teachers, parents and the university community, so it is only a fallback for travellers. Contact is through the group's phone, email and WhatsApp.
