The Best Hostels on the Camino Inglés

The Camino Inglés is one of the shorter Camino de Santiago routes, making it an attractive option for backpackers and young travellers who want to experience the Camino without committing to several weeks on the trail. This guide covers accommodation along the final stretch, starting in Ferrol and finishing in Sigüeiro, just outside Santiago de Compostela.

We've picked out the hostels and albergues that are worth considering, from traditional pilgrim accommodation to more sociable private hostels. Whether you're looking for somewhere to meet other travellers, a comfortable bed after a long day's walking, or simply a good-value place to stay, these are the places to check out.

Please note: The hostels do not pay us to be on this list!

  • Best place to stay before starting: Hostal La Frontera
  • Best alternative in Fene: O Albergue de Fene
  • Best traditional pilgrim albergue: Albergue de Peregrinos de Neda
  • Best hostel in Cabanas: Alda Cabanas Nature
  • Best modern hostel in Pontedeume: Albergue Río Eume
  • Best budget pilgrim albergue: Albergue de Peregrinos de Miño
  • Best private hostel in Betanzos: Albergue Río Mandeo
  • Best comfortable stay in Bruma: Hostal San Lorenzo de Bruma
  • Best final-night hostel: Albergue Segue o Camiño

Hostal La Frontera
Best place to stay before starting the Camino

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.1 / 10 (2,170 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 3.5 / 5 stars (655 reviews)

Best for: Travellers who want a private room in Ferrol before setting off on the Camino Inglés.

Hostal La Frontera is a centrally located guesthouse in Ferrol, making it a practical option if you're arriving in town before beginning your walk. It offers private rooms, Wi-Fi, a restaurant and cafeteria, laundry facilities and 24-hour reception. It's a more conventional hostal than a social backpacker hostel, but it provides a straightforward place to get organised before hitting the trail.

Not for you if: You're looking for a lively backpacker atmosphere, dorm accommodation or organised social activities.

  • Area: Central Ferrol
  • Address: Calle San Andrés 4, Ferrol, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Hostal La Frontera website
  • Phone: +34 881 953 036
  • Email: hostalafrontera@gmail.com

O Albergue de Fene
Best alternative accommodation in Fene

  • Booking.com Rating: 8 / 10 (467 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.2 / 5 stars (71 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims who want a modern, well-equipped place to stay, with a little more privacy than a traditional dormitory.

O Albergue de Fene offers a slightly different take on the traditional Camino albergue. Its accommodation is arranged in private cabins, with communal facilities including a kitchen, lounge and laundry. There are adapted bathrooms too, making it a useful option for travellers who need accessible facilities. It's around 400 metres from the Camino route.

Not for you if: You want a traditional large dormitory or a busy hostel bar and social scene.

  • Area: Fene
  • Address: Avenida Marqués de Figueroa, 4, Fene, A Coruña
  • Website: Official O Albergue de Fene website
  • Phone: +34 613 795 143
  • Email: info@alberguedefene.com

Albergue de Peregrinos de Neda
Best traditional pilgrim albergue

  • Google Places Rating: 4.5 (274 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: This is a traditional public pilgrim albergue rather than a commercial hostel, so it should be judged on its facilities and suitability for Camino walkers rather than OTA ratings.

Best for: Pilgrims who want the traditional communal albergue experience and a budget-friendly place to sleep.

The public pilgrim albergue in Neda is a straightforward stop for walkers following the Camino Inglés. It offers shared accommodation and communal facilities, and is intended specifically for pilgrims. You'll need your pilgrim credential, and advance reservations are not accepted. It's a practical option for anyone who wants to keep costs down and meet fellow walkers along the way. Note that you cannot book ahead for this hostel, it's on a first come, first served basis.

Not for you if: You prefer a private room, hotel-style facilities or the certainty of having a bed reserved in advance.

  • Area: Neda
  • Address: O Empedrón, s/n, Neda, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Camino de Santiago listing
  • Phone: +34 682 62 33 35
  • Email: albergueneda@neda.gal

Alda Cabanas Nature
Best hostel in Cabanas

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.6 / 10 (635 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 3.9 / 5 Stars (78 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims looking for a quieter, more comfortable albergue with communal facilities and the option of private accommodation.

Alda Cabanas Nature offers pilgrim accommodation in a natural setting near Cabanas. The albergue has shared sleeping accommodation, with private cabins also available. Facilities include a kitchen and communal area, and the property has accessible accommodation options. It's a useful alternative to the more basic public albergues along this stretch of the Camino. Note that this establishment is closed during the winter months - phone ahead to check if it is accepting guests

Not for you if: You want to stay right in the centre of town or are looking for a lively backpacker hostel with a bar and organised activities.

  • Area: O Pereiro, Laraxe, Cabanas
  • Address: Lugar Paraje Peón, 1, O Pereiro, Laraxe, Cabanas, A Coruña
  • Website: Alda Hotels direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 981 275 354
  • Email: cabanasnature@aldahotels.es

Albergue Río Eume
Best modern hostel in Pontedeume

  • Google Places Rating: 4.6 / 5 Stars (388 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims who appreciate modern facilities, a comfortable communal space and the option of private accommodation.

Albergue Río Eume is a modern private albergue in Pontedeume. It offers shared and private accommodation, with a kitchen, common room, Wi-Fi and laundry facilities. It's a good option if you'd prefer something a little more comfortable than a basic municipal albergue, while still having the opportunity to meet other Camino walkers. Note that this establishment closes during the winter months - phone ahead to check if they're accepting guests.

Not for you if: You're looking for a large party hostel or a traditional, no-frills municipal albergue.

  • Area: Pontedeume
  • Address: Rúa Club Firrete, 21 bajo, 15600 Pontedeume, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Albergue Río Eume website
  • Phone: +34 604 036 109
  • Email: alberguerioeume@gmail.com

Albergue de Peregrinos de Miño
Best budget pilgrim albergue

  • Google Places Rating: 4.4 / 5 Stars (227 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: This is a public pilgrim albergue, not a commercial hostel. Its appeal is its practical facilities and budget-friendly accommodation rather than hotel-style comforts.

Best for: Budget-conscious pilgrims who want a traditional shared albergue and don't need private accommodation.

The public albergue in Miño provides simple accommodation for pilgrims walking the Camino Inglés. It has 22 beds and communal facilities, and is a useful option for keeping accommodation costs down. As with other public pilgrim albergues, you'll need your pilgrim credential and cannot reserve a bed in advance.

Not for you if: You want to guarantee a bed before arriving, or prefer private rooms and more extensive facilities.

  • Area: Miño
  • Address: Rúa Marismas, s/n, 15630 Miño, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Camino de Santiago listing
  • Phone: +34 689 233 678
  • Email: turismomino@gmail.com

Albergue Río Mandeo
Best private hostel in Betanzos

  • Google Places Rating: 4.4 / 5 Stars (298 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims who want modern accommodation in Betanzos, with shared facilities and the option of a private room.

Albergue Río Mandeo is a modern private albergue in Betanzos, offering both shared and private accommodation. Facilities include a kitchen, common room, Wi-Fi and laundry facilities. It's a convenient option for walkers who want to spend some time exploring Betanzos without sacrificing the practical comforts of a well-equipped hostel. Note that this establishment is closed during the winter months - contact them ahead of time to find out if they're accepting guests.

Not for you if: You're looking for a lively party hostel or a traditional public albergue with the lowest possible nightly cost.

  • Area: Betanzos
  • Address: Rúa Doctor Fleming, 3, 15300 Betanzos, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Albergue Río Mandeo website
  • Phone: +34 604 044 448
  • Email: albergueriomandeo@gmail.com

Hostal San Lorenzo de Bruma
Best comfortable stay in Bruma

  • Google Places Rating: 4.6 / 5 Stars (502 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims who want a comfortable private-room option in Bruma, particularly after a long day on the trail.

Hostal San Lorenzo de Bruma is a more comfortable alternative to a traditional pilgrim dormitory. The renovated property offers rooms with private bathrooms, along with a shared lounge, laundry facilities, luggage storage and an outdoor garden and terrace. It's a good option if you'd like a little more comfort at one of the Camino's quieter overnight stops.

Not for you if: You're travelling on the tightest possible budget or specifically want a sociable dormitory-style hostel.

  • Area: Bruma
  • Address: Calle Hospital de Bruma, 9, 15865 Mesía, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Hostal San Lorenzo de Bruma website
  • Phone: +34 619 464 240
  • Email: info@alberguesanlorenzodebruma.com

Albergue Segue o Camiño
Best final-night hostel

  • Booking.com Rating: 9.3 / 10 (1,627 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.9 / 5 Stars (717 reviews)

Best for: Pilgrims who want a modern, bookable hostel in Sigüeiro before completing the final walk into Santiago.

Albergue Segue o Camiño is a modern private albergue in Sigüeiro, with shared dormitories and a private suite. Facilities include air conditioning, lockers and basic kitchen amenities such as a microwave, fridge and facilities for heating water. It's a convenient place to spend the night before tackling the final stretch of the Camino into Santiago de Compostela.

Not for you if: You want a fully equipped self-catering kitchen or a hostel with a bar and a big social programme.

  • Area: Sigüeiro
  • Address: Rúa Camiño Inglés, 6, bajo, 15888 Sigüeiro, Oroso, A Coruña
  • Website: Official Albergue Segue o Camiño website
  • Phone: +34 613 379 963
  • Email: info@segueocamino.com
