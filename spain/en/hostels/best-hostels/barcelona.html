The Best Hostels in Barcelona

Ratings, prices and availability checked: 7 November 2026.

Barcelona is one of Europe’s great backpacker cities: cheap flights, beach days, Gaudí, late dinners, and a hostel scene built to turn solo travellers into a travel crew by the second night. The best hostel for you depends less on star ratings and more on one question: what kind of fun are you here for?

If you want to party until sunrise, Kabul Party Hostel is the obvious choice. If you want to meet people without sacrificing sleep, choose Sant Jordi Rock Palace or Yeah Barcelona. If you are travelling alone and want a ready-made group, Onefam Sants or Onefam Paralelo are the strongest picks.

This guide covers the best party hostels, social hostels, budget picks and beach stays in Barcelona, with neighbourhood advice, typical prices, booking tips and direct hostel contact details. For the complete accommodation directory, see our full Barcelona backpacking guide.

How we chose these hostels

We compared Barcelona hostels using guest ratings across Booking.com, Hostelworld and Google, as well as location, social atmosphere, facilities, transport links, price, and direct booking options. We prioritised hostels that make sense for backpackers: secure dorms, social events, useful locations, and transparent contact details.

We include direct contact details because not everyone wants to book through an affiliate platform. Prices, availability and ratings change, so always confirm directly with the hostel before booking.

Best Party Hostels in Barcelona

Barcelona’s party hostels are not subtle. They are for backpackers who want pre-drinks on a rooftop, a pub crawl with thirty new friends, and an alarm clock that is technically optional. Here are the best ones, ranked by the type of night out they create.

Kabul Party Hostel
Best overall party hostel

  • Booking.com Rating: 8.5 / 10 (3,916 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.5 / 10 (11,647 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.5 / 5 Stars (2,733 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Kabul’s exceptionally high Hostelworld score reflects its social atmosphere and location, but recent reviews also mention noise and variable cleanliness—so it is best for party-focused backpackers rather than light sleepers.

Best for: Backpackers who want to be in the middle of Barcelona’s nightlife from the moment they arrive.

Kabul sits directly on Plaça Reial in the Gothic Quarter, just off La Rambla. It has a rooftop terrace, bar, pool table, free breakfast, daily walking tours and a nightly pub crawl. If your priority is location, atmosphere and meeting people who are also there to party, this is the hostel to book.

Not for you if: you are a light sleeper or want a quiet, polished hostel. This is a full-send party hostel.

  • Area: Gothic Quarter / Plaça Reial
  • Nearest metro: Liceu (L3)
  • Address: Plaça Reial 17, 08002 Barcelona

Onefam Sants
Best hostel for solo travellers

  • Booking.com Rating: 9.3 / 10 (3,201 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.9 / 10 (8,116 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.5 / 5 Stars (1,058 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Onefam Sungate’s near-perfect Hostelworld rating reflects its welcoming staff, central location and unusually social atmosphere. Free dinners, walking tours and pub crawls make it especially good for solo travellers who want to meet people quickly, while the lack of bunk beds makes it a more comfortable choice than many party hostels.

Best for: First-time solo backpackers who want friends before they have unpacked.

Onefam Sants is less about being beside every bar and more about making sure you never spend the night alone. Expect free dinners, pub crawls, club-entry perks and daily activities. It is near Sants station, which makes arrivals, departures and airport connections easy.

Not for you if: you want to stumble home from a club in two minutes. The Gothic Quarter is roughly 20 minutes away.

Sant Jordi Hostels Rock Palace
Best party hostel with a pool

  • Booking.com Rating: 8.4 / 10 (4,079 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.1 / 10 (8,602 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.5 / 5 Stars (1,952 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Sant Jordi Rock Palace’s strong ratings reflect its lively social atmosphere, friendly staff, central Eixample location and standout rooftop pool. Guests consistently praise the pub crawls, organised events and easy-going vibe, making it a better choice than a hardcore party hostel if you want to meet people and enjoy Barcelona’s nightlife without sacrificing comfort or sleep.

Best for: Backpackers who want a proper party hostel but also a rooftop pool, better rooms and a little more comfort.

Rock Palace is in Eixample near Passeig de Gràcia, with a seasonal rooftop pool, music-themed interiors, karaoke, a bar and staff-led pub and club tours. It is the best compromise if you want the party but do not want to feel like you are sleeping inside a nightclub.

Not for you if: you are visiting in winter and specifically want to use the pool. The pool is seasonal and daytime only.

  • Area: Eixample, near Passeig de Gràcia
  • Nearest metro: Passeig de Gràcia (L2, L3, L4)

Onefam Paralelo
Best cheap party hostel in Poble Sec

  • Booking.com Rating: 9.5 / 10 (1,200 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.9 / 10 (8,599)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.6 / 5 Stars (694 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Onefam Paralelo’s near-perfect Hostelworld rating reflects its exceptionally social atmosphere, friendly staff and strong value for money. Free dinners, daily activities, walking tours and pub crawls make it one of Barcelona’s best hostels for solo travellers who want to meet people quickly, while its Poble Sec location keeps it close to the nightlife without the noise and expense of the Gothic Quarter.

Best for: Budget backpackers who want free food, cheap drinks and a club night without paying Gothic Quarter prices.

Onefam Paralelo is in Poble Sec, close to Sala Apolo and the cheap pintxos bars of Carrer de Blai. It has a party room, lounge, coworking space and nightly activities. The free dinner is the big draw: eat first, then join the hostel night out.

Yeah Barcelona is known for nightly group dinners, bar crawls and walking tours. It has a cleaner, more contemporary feel than the older party institutions, making it ideal if you want new friends and nights out but still want to sleep reasonably well.

Best hostel for activities and local vibes

What the ratings tell you: Pars Tailor’s strong ratings reflect its friendly staff, social atmosphere and unusually varied activity programme. Family dinners, paella nights, walking tours, beach days, bar crawls and club nights make it a great choice for solo travellers who want to meet people, while the Sant Antoni location offers a more local, less touristy base than the Gothic Quarter.

Best for: Backpackers who want to party, but also want beach volleyball, sunsets, paella and a more local Barcelona experience.

Pars Tailor is in Sant Antoni, a great neighbourhood for cheaper food, local bars and a less touristy feel. Activities include beach volleyball, sunset trips to Bunkers del Carmel, Magic Fountain visits, paella classes, pub crawls and club nights.

St Christopher’s Inn Barcelona
Best hostel for groups

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.7 / 10 (6,481 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 8.7 / 10 (15,950 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.2 / 5 Stars (5,187 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: St Christopher’s Inn’s ratings reflect its unbeatable central location, friendly staff and lively social scene rather than a boutique-hostel experience. Belushi’s bar, free welcome events, walking tours and organised nights out make it easy to meet people, while the private dorm curtains, 24-hour reception and proximity to Plaça Catalunya make it a practical base for groups and first-time visitors.

Best for: Groups of friends, stag and hen parties, and travellers who want a bar underneath their hostel.

St Christopher’s is beside Plaça Catalunya, at the top of La Rambla, with Belushi’s bar downstairs. It is big, social and built for groups, with private rooms, dorms, female-only rooms, a 24-hour reception, live sport and organised pub crawls.

Where to Stay in Barcelona for Nightlife

Gothic Quarter and El Raval are best if you want to be in the middle of everything: La Rambla, backpacker bars, tapas, clubs and the city’s most atmospheric streets. Kabul and Itaca are strong picks here.

El Born is better for cocktail bars, live music and a slightly more stylish night out. It is social without being as relentless as the Gothic Quarter.

Poble Sec is the best neighbourhood for backpackers who want good-value food, local bars and Sala Apolo nearby. Onefam Paralelo is the obvious hostel choice.

Eixample is the best base for a balance of sightseeing, transport, restaurants and social hostels. Sant Jordi Rock Palace, Yeah Barcelona and Onefam Batlló are all in or near this area.

Barceloneta and Poblenou are best for beach days, rooftop afternoons and summer nightlife. Expect more travel time to the Gothic Quarter, but better access to the beach and Port Olímpic clubs.

How Much Do Barcelona Hostels Cost?

Most dorm beds in Barcelona cost roughly €15–€35 per night, depending on the season, room size and how early you book. August, festival periods and major conference weeks can push prices much higher.

Also budget for Barcelona’s tourist tax. Since April 2026, hostel guests pay approximately €6 per person per night in combined regional and municipal tourist taxes, usually paid at the hostel rather than included in online booking prices.

For a three-night stay, a realistic backpacker budget is around €200–€300 including a dorm bed, tourist tax, food, drinks, transport and a couple of nights out.

Barcelona Hostel FAQs

What is the best party hostel in Barcelona?

Kabul Party Hostel is the best party hostel in Barcelona for most backpackers because of its Plaça Reial location, rooftop terrace, bar, free breakfast and nightly pub crawl.

What is the best hostel in Barcelona for solo travellers?

Onefam Sants is the best choice for solo travellers who want a built-in social group. Onefam Paralelo is a great alternative if you prefer a smaller hostel near Poble Sec nightlife.

What is the best area to stay in Barcelona for backpackers?

The Gothic Quarter, El Raval and El Born are best for central nightlife and sightseeing. Poble Sec is better for cheaper food and a more local bar scene, while Eixample is best for comfort and transport.

How much is a hostel in Barcelona?

Most dorm beds cost around €15–€35 per night, with higher prices in summer and during festivals. Add roughly €6 per person per night for Barcelona’s tourist tax.

Are Barcelona hostels safe?

Yes. Violent crime is rare and most hostels have lockers and controlled access. The main risk is pickpocketing, especially around La Rambla, the metro and busy nightlife areas.

When should I book a hostel in Barcelona?

Book as early as possible for May to September, La Mercè, Primavera Sound, Sónar and weekend dates. The best social hostels sell out first.
