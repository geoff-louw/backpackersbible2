The Best Hostels in Bilbao

Ratings, prices and availability checked: 27 November 2026.

Bilbao is the Basque Country’s big, gritty, good-looking city: the Guggenheim, the seven medieval streets of the Casco Viejo, pintxo bars on every corner, Athletic Club match days and a metro that runs straight to the surf beaches. It is smaller and calmer than Barcelona or Madrid, and its hostel scene reflects that. Do not expect sunrise party hostels. Expect boutique hostels, good beds, and a pintxo crawl as the main event.

If you want the best all-round hostel with a bar and nightly events, book Latroupe La Granja. If you want to roll out of bed into the Casco Viejo pintxo bars, choose Quartier Bilbao. If the Guggenheim is the reason you came, Poshtel Bilbao is right beside it. On a tight budget, Bilbao Akelarre Hostel in Deusto is the best value, and women travelling solo should look at Lamia BBK Women Hostel.

This guide covers the best hostels in Bilbao for social travellers, budget backpackers, Camino pilgrims and solo female travellers, with neighbourhood advice, typical prices, the new tourist tax, booking tips and direct hostel contact details. For the complete accommodation directory, see our full Basque Country hostel directory.

How we chose these hostels

We compared Bilbao hostels using guest ratings across Booking.com, Hostelworld and Google, as well as location, social atmosphere, facilities, transport links, price, and direct booking options. We prioritised hostels that make sense for backpackers: secure dorms, social spaces, useful locations, and transparent contact details. Where a hostel has weak scores in a particular area, we tell you.

We include direct contact details because not everyone wants to book through an affiliate platform. Prices, availability and ratings change, so always confirm directly with the hostel before booking.

  • Best overall hostel in Bilbao: Latroupe La Granja
  • Best hostel in the Casco Viejo: Quartier Bilbao Hostel
  • Best hostel near the Guggenheim: Poshtel Bilbao
  • Best budget hostel: Bilbao Akelarre Hostel

Our Top Hostel Picks in Bilbao

Bilbao’s hostels are less about chaos and more about comfort, location and a good night out on pintxos and txakoli. Here are the best ones, ranked by the kind of trip they suit.

Latroupe La Granja
Best overall hostel in Bilbao

  • Booking.com Rating: 8.8 / 10 (6,275 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.3 / 10 (1,409 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.6 / 5 Stars (1,585 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Latroupe La Granja is the highest-rated hostel in Bilbao by a clear margin. Its Hostelworld scores for location (9.7), staff (9.6), security (9.6) and cleanliness (9.4) are all excellent, and guests regularly mention the privacy curtains, comfortable beds and spotless bathrooms. Atmosphere is its lowest score (8.6), which tells you this is a lively boutique hostel rather than a hardcore party hostel.

Best for: Almost everyone: solo travellers, couples, friends and digital nomads who want a central, comfortable base with a built-in social life.

La Granja sits in a grand old building on Plaza Circular, in the Abando district, with the metro directly outside and Bilbao-Abando train station next door. The basement bar hosts daily events such as karaoke, movie nights, games, meet-and-greets and themed evenings, and there are walking tours in English and Spanish. All dorms (4, 5, 6 and 8 beds, plus female-only dorms for 4 or 6) have their own en-suite bathroom, air conditioning and lockers. There is also a guest kitchen, coworking space, laundry and a buffet breakfast (check whether it is included in your rate).

Not for you if: you are looking for the cheapest bed in town. It is one of the pricier hostels in Bilbao, and towels cost extra in the dorms.

  • Area: Abando / Plaza Circular
  • Nearest metro: Abando (L1, L2)
  • Address: Plaza Circular, 3, Abando, 48001 Bilbao
  • Website: Official Latroupe La Granja website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 944 69 00 44
  • WhatsApp: +34 601 80 50 89
  • Email: hellolagranja@latroupe.com

Quartier Bilbao Hostel
Best hostel in the Casco Viejo

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.4 / 10 (2,245 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 7.3 / 10 (1,802 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.1 / 5 Stars (728 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Quartier’s scores are all about location. Its Hostelworld location score is 9.4 and guests consistently praise the friendly staff (8.3) and the free breakfast. The weak spot is cleanliness (5.7 on Hostelworld), with recent reviews mentioning tired bathrooms, so read the latest reviews before you book and pack flip-flops for the showers.

Best for: Pintxo hunters and first-time visitors who want to be in the middle of the old town for the lowest possible effort.

Quartier is on Artekale, one of the original Siete Calles (seven streets) of the Casco Viejo, a minute from La Ribera market, the river and Santiago Cathedral, and a short walk from Plaza Nueva’s pintxo bars. There is a self-service continental breakfast, a shared kitchen, bunk beds with reading lights and lockers, and the hostel organises pub crawls, live concerts and yoga sessions. It also has a long track record with large groups.

Not for you if: cleanliness and modern bathrooms are your top priority. Latroupe La Granja is a 10-minute walk away and scores far higher.

  • Area: Casco Viejo (old town)
  • Nearest metro: Casco Viejo (L1, L2), about 5 minutes on foot
  • Address: Artekale, 15, Ibaiondo, 48005 Bilbao
  • Website: Official Quartier Bilbao website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 944 97 88 00
  • Email: recepcion@quartierbilbao.com

Poshtel Bilbao
Best hostel near the Guggenheim

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.6 / 10 (2,473 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 7.8 / 10 (1,188 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 3.9 / 5 Stars (875 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Poshtel’s location score (9.3 on Hostelworld) and security score (8.9) are its big strengths, and many guests love the design and common areas. Atmosphere (6.7) and facilities (6.9) are lower, and some reviews mention large dorms and a lack of power sockets, so it is better as a stylish, central base than as a place to make a crew of new friends.

Best for: Art and architecture fans who want to wake up beside the Guggenheim and still walk to the old town.

Poshtel calls itself Bilbao’s first premium hostel. It is on Calle Heros in the Ensanche, a short walk from the Guggenheim, the riverside promenade, Gran Vía and the Casco Viejo. Choose a bunk in a shared dorm (each bed with its own locker) or a private room, with free fast Wi-Fi, design-led interiors, a programme of activities and its own bar-restaurant, Txío. Some pet-friendly rooms are available on request.

Not for you if: you want a big social scene or cooking facilities. This is more boutique hotel with bunks than backpacker hangout.

  • Area: Ensanche / Abando, near the Guggenheim and the river
  • Nearest metro: Moyua (L1, L2); Uribitarte tram stop is also close
  • Address: Heros Kalea, 7, Abando, 48009 Bilbao
  • Website: Official Poshtel Bilbao website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 944 25 65 86
  • Email: No public email; use the contact form on the hostel’s website

Bilbao Akelarre Hostel
Best budget hostel in Bilbao

  • Booking.com Rating: 8.2 / 10 (1,040 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: Not currently listed on Hostelworld
  • Google Places Rating: 4.3 / 5 Stars (349 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Akelarre’s Booking.com scores are consistent across the board, with staff (8.6), cleanliness (8.5) and value for money (8.4) leading the way. It is a small, friendly, quiet hostel rather than a social hub, and the trade-off for the lower price is a location outside the centre.

Best for: Budget backpackers, light sleepers and Camino del Norte walkers who want a clean, cheap bed and do not mind a short metro ride.

Akelarre is in Deusto, Bilbao’s university district, just across the river from the Guggenheim (about 2 km away) and close to the Euskalduna concert hall and San Mamés stadium. It has been running since 2009, with around 38 beds in dorms of 2 to 12, a 6-bed female dorm, family and double rooms, individual lockers, bike storage, a microwave and fridge, free tea and coffee all day, and 24-hour reception. Deusto itself has cheap bars and plenty of student life.

Not for you if: you want to walk out of the door into the Casco Viejo or meet people over organised events.

  • Area: Deusto
  • Nearest metro: Deusto (L1, L2)
  • Address: Morgan Kalea, 4-6, Deusto, 48014 Bilbao
  • Website: Official Bilbao Akelarre Hostel website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 944 05 77 13
  • Email: info@bilbaoakelarrehostel.com

All Iron Hostel
Best hostel for Camino pilgrims and groups

  • Booking.com Rating: 7.7 / 10 (3,192 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 7.7 / 10 (614 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 3.9 / 5 Stars (786 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: All Iron’s ratings are solid rather than spectacular, with friendly staff (8.3) and good cleanliness (8.1) on Hostelworld. Atmosphere (6.8) and location (7.8) are lower because it is up on a hill outside the old town, but the free breakfast and terrace views make it good value.

Best for: Camino del Norte pilgrims, groups and road-trippers who want breakfast included and free parking.

All Iron sits on the Artagan hill near the Basilica of Begoña, right on the historic northern route of the Camino de Santiago and about eight minutes from the Casco Viejo. The name nods to the iron mining that built Bilbao. It has dorms for 4, 6, 8 and 10, a pilgrims’ room, a female-only room, an adapted room and private rooms, all facing outside. Breakfast is included, and there is a fully equipped kitchen, a games room, a terrace with panoramic city views, 24-hour reception, and social events such as game nights and tours. Groups of 50 or more get special rates, with free parking for cars and coaches.

  • Area: Begoña / Santutxu (Artagan hill)
  • Nearest metro: Santutxu (L3), about 7 minutes on foot
  • Address: Jose Maria Ugarteburu Kalea, 2, 48007 Bilbao
  • Website: Official All Iron Hostel website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 944 03 65 34
  • Email: info@allironhostel.com

Lamia BBK Women Hostel
Best hostel for female travellers

  • Booking.com Rating: 8.8 / 10 (520 reviews)
  • Hostelworld.com Rating: 9.2 / 10 (108 reviews)
  • Google Places Rating: 4.4 / 5 Stars (39 reviews)

What the ratings tell you: Lamia’s Hostelworld scores for security (9.8), value for money (9.6), staff (9.6) and cleanliness (9.4) are among the best in the city. Location is the one weaker score (7.9), because it is in a residential district outside the centre, but it is well connected by bus and metro.

Best for: Women travelling solo, with friends or in groups who want a safe, spotless and genuinely welcoming base.

Lamia is a women-only hostel, which it says is the only one in Bilbao. It is in the Rekalde district, with the entrance through the rear courtyard on Altube Kalea 2, and is about 15 minutes on foot from San Mamés and the Intermodal bus station. Shared rooms range from 4 to 12 beds, with private and adapted rooms too. Every room has lockers, a personal door code, heating and air conditioning, and guests share a fully equipped kitchen, a lounge with a library and games, and the “Mari” hall, which hosts community, wellbeing and cultural activities.

Not for you if: you are travelling with male friends or want to stay in the centre. Check-in hours are also limited (roughly 15:00 to 19:00 or 20:00), so plan late arrivals in advance.

  • Area: Rekalde
  • Nearest metro: San Mamés (L1, L2), about 15 minutes on foot; buses run to the Guggenheim and Abando
  • Address: Gordóniz Kalea, 66 (entrance via Altube Kalea, 2), 48002 Bilbao
  • Website: Official Lamia BBK Women Hostel website / direct bookings
  • Phone: +34 946 62 07 38
  • Email: lamia@lamiabbkhostel.com

Where to Stay in Bilbao

Casco Viejo is the best area if you want pintxo bars, txakoli, Plaza Nueva and the old town’s nightlife on your doorstep. Quartier Bilbao is the obvious hostel pick here.

Abando and the Ensanche are the best all-round base: central, well connected by metro and train, and a short walk from both the Casco Viejo and the Guggenheim. Latroupe La Granja and Poshtel Bilbao are both here.

Bilbao La Vieja and San Francisco, just across the river from the old town, are the city’s alternative quarter, with cheaper bars, street art and live music. It is lively at night, so keep your wits about you when walking home late.

Deusto is the student district across the river from the Guggenheim. It is quieter and cheaper, with good metro links. Bilbao Akelarre Hostel is here.

Begoña and Santutxu sit on the hill above the Casco Viejo, on the Camino del Norte route, with great views and a more residential feel. All Iron Hostel is here.

How Much Do Bilbao Hostels Cost?

Most dorm beds in Bilbao cost roughly €18–€35 per night, depending on the season, room size and how early you book. Prices jump during BBK Live in July, Aste Nagusia (Bilbao’s big summer fiesta) in August, and on big Athletic Club match days.

Also budget for Bilbao’s new tourist tax. From 1 January 2027, hostel guests are due to pay about €1 per person per night, for a maximum of six nights per stay, with under-18s exempt. The bylaw was provisionally approved in September 2026, so check with your hostel for the final details.

For a three-night stay, a realistic backpacker budget is around €180–€260 including a dorm bed, food, a pintxo crawl or two, transport and a visit to the Guggenheim.

Bilbao Hostel FAQs

What is the best hostel in Bilbao?

Latroupe La Granja is the best hostel in Bilbao for most backpackers. It has the highest guest ratings in the city, a central location on Plaza Circular, en-suite dorms, and a bar with daily events.

Is there a party hostel in Bilbao?

Not in the Barcelona sense. Bilbao’s nightlife centres on pintxo crawls in the Casco Viejo and bars in Bilbao La Vieja rather than hostel parties. Latroupe La Granja is the most social option, with its own bar and nightly events, and Quartier Bilbao runs pub crawls.

What is the best area to stay in Bilbao for backpackers?

The Casco Viejo is best for pintxos and nightlife, while Abando and the Ensanche are best for transport and easy access to both the old town and the Guggenheim. Deusto is a good option if you want to spend less.

How much is a hostel in Bilbao?

Most dorm beds cost around €18–€35 per night, with higher prices in July and August. From January 2027, add roughly €1 per person per night for Bilbao’s tourist tax.

Are Bilbao hostels safe?

Yes. Bilbao is one of Spain’s safer cities and most hostels have lockers and controlled access. Take normal care with your belongings in busy areas, and at night in parts of Bilbao La Vieja and San Francisco.

Which Bilbao hostels are good for Camino del Norte pilgrims?

All Iron Hostel is right on the Camino del Norte route near the Basilica of Begoña and has a room set aside for pilgrims. Bilbao Akelarre Hostel in Deusto is a good-value alternative with bike storage.

When should I book a hostel in Bilbao?

Book as early as possible for July and August, especially during BBK Live and Aste Nagusia, and for weekends when Athletic Club are playing at home. Latroupe La Granja and Lamia BBK have the fewest cheap beds and tend to fill first.
