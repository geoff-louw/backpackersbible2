Budget Accommodation In Asturias

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

ALBERGUE LA ESTACIÓN

STREET ADDRESS: C. Román Romano, 15, 33500 Llanes, Asturias

PHONE: +34 985 97 49 10

WEBSITE: alberguelaestacionllanes.com

EMAIL: alberguelaestacionllanes@gmail.com

GOOGLE RATING: 4.3 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Albergue La Estación occupies the old FEVE railway station buildings in the town of Llanes, on the Camino de Santiago del Norte. It is a privately run albergue, classed as first category under Asturian tourist-hostel rules, so you do not need a pilgrim's credential to stay. Guests have use of a kitchen with a ceramic hob and oven, a washing machine, a tumble dryer and free Wi-Fi. Llanes is a municipality of tourist excellence with more than 30 beaches, about 40 km from the Picos de Europa National Park and 30 km from the Sella and Cares-Deva salmon rivers. That makes it a handy base for hiking, kayaking, canyoning, climbing and mountain biking as well as a stop for pilgrims. The albergue's website mentions a March to October season, so check opening dates before you travel. The Booking.com and Hostelworld listings have photos and reviews.

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Boogalow is a mini hostel in the centre of Gijón, directly opposite the beach, with just seven rooms, each one different. The view is what guests value most: you wake up looking over the sea, the beach and the old town. There are two shared spaces, a fully equipped kitchen and a lounge with games and books, plus free coffee, hot chocolate and herbal tea all day, a washing machine and a dryer. Sheets, towels, shampoo and shower gel, ear plugs and fast Wi-Fi are provided, and the hostel has a paddle-surf board, a bodyboard and skateboards. The atmosphere is described as quiet, mixed and respectful, so expect several languages and the odd child's laugh. In high season reception is staffed until 17:00; at other times check-in is handled online and the latest arrival is 21:00. Shops, restaurants and nightlife are close by.

PHONE: +34 985 87 75 94

EMAIL: info@gijonsurfhostel.com

WHATSAPP: +34 985 87 75 94

GOOGLE RATING: 4.5 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Gijón Surf Hostel is a surf-themed hostel on Camino de las Higueras, around 150 to 200 metres from El Rinconín beach, separated from the sand by a green area. It doubles as an official surf school, so surf and paddle-surf lessons are on offer and boards and snorkelling equipment can be hired on site. Guests share a kitchen and a lounge, and there is a garden with barbecue facilities, free Wi-Fi, free parking, table tennis, billiards, darts and bike hire. It is registered as a first-category tourist hostel with 12 beds, which keeps it small and sociable. It is also listed by the regional tourism board for walkers on the Camino de la Costa, with secure bike storage and public transport within 500 metres. Hostelworld guests score it 9.0 from 173 reviews, and Booking.com reviewers mention the friendly community, daily cleaning and fun lessons.

GREEN HOSTEL

STREET ADDRESS: C. Magdalena, 3, 2º izquierda, 33009 Oviedo, Asturias

PHONE: +34 985 02 26 68

WEBSITE: greenhostel.es

EMAIL: info@greenhostel.es

FACEBOOK: facebook.com/greenhosteloviedo

GOOGLE RATING: 4.6 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Green Hostel is a modern, sustainability-minded hostel in the historic centre of Oviedo, on Calle Magdalena next to the Plaza del Ayuntamiento. It occupies the former Hostal Arcos, which for decades was a well-known base for visitors to the city. There are shared and private rooms, a terrace and common areas designed so guests can swap stories and tips. It is registered as a first-category tourist hostel with 26 beds and is open all year, with breakfast included according to its official tourism-board listing. Heating, internet access, a washing machine and a laundry service are available and cards are accepted. The owners aim to offer extras such as guided tours, outdoor activities, cider-house tastings and bike routes, and they work to cut waste, water and electricity use. It is not pilgrim-only, but it sits on the first stage of the Camino Primitivo out of Oviedo.

EMAIL: oviedo@hihomehostel.com

GOOGLE RATING: 3.9 out of 5 stars

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

HiHome Hostel is a small hostel on Calle Uría in the centre of Oviedo, close to Plaza de España and about a one-minute walk from the train station. It has six rooms: two doubles, two triples and two shared rooms with bunk beds, so it suits couples and small groups as well as solo travellers. Check-in runs from 14:00 until midnight and check-out is before 12:00. The staff speak Spanish, English and French. It is open all year and you do not need a pilgrim's credential to stay, which makes it a convenient stop for walkers starting or finishing the Camino Primitivo, which begins at Oviedo's cathedral. Booking ahead is advisable. Pets are not accepted. Review sites put its guest score at around 7.8 out of 10, and the Booking.com and Hostelworld listings have photos and guest reviews.

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

HiHome Hostel Catedral opened in 2025 on the first floor of a building at Calle San Antonio 7, in the heart of Oviedo and close to both the cathedral and the railway station. It is the second HiHome hostel in the city, sharing its website and contact details with HiHome Hostel on Calle Uría, and the staff speak Spanish, English and French. Hostelworld describes it as a modern, welcoming hostel, and a regional pilgrim directory says it has all the comforts a pilgrim might need. The cathedral is the starting point of the Camino Primitivo, so the location suits pilgrims setting out on the route, and the hostel also takes tourists and other travellers. The Booking.com and Hostelworld listings have photos and guest reviews, and as it is a new property it is worth checking the most recent ones.

HOTEL LA CAMPANA

STREET ADDRESS: AS-381, km 3, 15, 33192 Pruvia de abajo, Asturias

PHONE: +34 985 96 47 83

WEBSITE: lacampana.net

EMAIL: hotel@lacampana.net

GOOGLE RATING: 4.2 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Hotel La Campana is a three-star hotel at Pruvia, on the AS-18, in the middle of Asturias. Its website puts it about 13 km from Oviedo's old town, 17 km from the beaches of Gijón and 44 km from Asturias airport, with Oviedo ten minutes away by road, Gijón twelve and Avilés twenty, which makes it a practical base for exploring the region by car. Rooms are doubles with one bed or two, superior doubles and a suite. There is plenty of parking, and guests mention the quiet, clean rooms, easy access and views over the garden. Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is at 12:00, and a small meeting room has a screen and projector. This is a conventional hotel with private rooms rather than dorms, so solo budget travellers may prefer the hostels in Oviedo or Gijón.

LA HOSPEDERÍA OVIEDO

STREET ADDRESS: C. Schultz, 7, bajo, 33003 Oviedo, Asturias

PHONE: +34 630 80 65 87

WEBSITE: lahospederiaoviedo.es

EMAIL: lahospederiaoviedo@gmail.com

WHATSAPP: +34 630 80 65 87

GOOGLE RATING: 4.5 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

La Hospedería is a small private albergue in Oviedo's old town, on Calle Schultz, a few metres from the cathedral and at kilometre zero of the first stage of the Camino Primitivo. That makes it a natural first or last night on the route, and its website opens with the old pilgrims' saying that whoever goes to Santiago but not to El Salvador visits the servant and not the lord, a reference to Oviedo's cathedral. Guests get individual lockers with keys, a private inner courtyard for bicycles, keyless entry with an access code and online check-in. The team gives pilgrims information and help to discover the city. Booking is through Booking.com via the albergue's website, and you can also contact it directly by email or phone if you have questions. Prices vary with the season, so check the booking pages for current rates and guest reviews.
