Budget Accommodation In Barcelona

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

360 Hostel Borne is at Ronda de Sant Pere 56, beside the El Borne neighbourhood, known for arts and culture, nightlife and restaurants, and a few blocks from Plaça Catalunya, La Rambla and Passeig de Gràcia. The hostel says it was awarded best hostel in the world for solo travellers, and it combines some calm with daily activities such as walking tours, free dinners and nights out. Its rooms are described as very spacious: a two-bed room with a private terrace and dorms of six, eight and twelve beds, all with free lockers, reading lights, air conditioning and linen. There is a well-equipped kitchen and a big terrace for the Barcelona sun. Reception is open 24 hours, check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is before 11:00, with free Wi-Fi throughout. Towels can be hired and laundry costs extra. Wheelchair access is listed among its facilities, and a pub crawl runs most nights.

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

360 Hostel Centro is a social hostel at Carrer de Bailèn 7 in central Barcelona, a few blocks from Plaça Catalunya, La Rambla and Passeig de Gràcia, with the Old Town next door and the Sagrada Família and the beach within walking distance. It has a big communal lounge with sofas and a large, fully equipped kitchen with a stove, microwave, toaster, fridge, pots and pans. Dorms sleep four, six or eight, and there is a two-bed room with its own terrace; every room has free lockers, reading lights, air conditioning and linen. Reception is open 24 hours, check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is before 11:00, and free Wi-Fi covers the building. The young staff run a free walking tour every day and a different activity each night, such as paella night, free sangria and tapas, pasta night and a pub crawl. Towels can be hired and laundry costs extra. The hostel is wheelchair accessible.
