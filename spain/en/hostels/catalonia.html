Budget Accommodation In Catalonia

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

ALBERG BANYOLES

STREET ADDRESS: Carrer Migdia, 10, 17820 Banyoles, Girona

PHONE: +34 972 57 54 54

Booking.com

Alberg de Banyoles is a tourist hostel at Carrer del Migdia 10 in the urban centre of Banyoles, in the Pla de l'Estany, aimed mainly at school groups, sports teams and families. Its site says the central position is ideal for sports stays in canoeing, swimming and rowing, with good access to Banyoles lake and the facilities of the Club Natació Banyoles, as well as excursions and activities for school classes and families. There are more than ten rooms with bathrooms for four to six people, and stays are on a full-board basis with educational, sporting and cultural activities. Families can combine a stay with a free guided visit and discount coupons for local activities. The Girona to Banyoles to Serinyà greenway, nearly 40 km of the Tren Pinxo route, is a new cycling option on the doorstep. A virtual tour of the hostel is on its website. Phone, email, Facebook and Instagram are listed in the footer.

Booking.com

La Molina Xanascat is a mountain hostel at 1,450 metres in the municipality of Alp, in the Cerdanya, built in stone and wood to blend into its surroundings. It is five minutes from the La Molina ski slopes, 15 from La Masella and about 25 from French resorts such as Font-Romeu, and Puigcerdà, the capital of the Cerdanya, is close. In winter it is a base for skiing and other mountain sports, and nearby you can hike in the Cadí-Moixeró Natural Park, visit the Martinet bunkers, see animals at a farm in Prullans or tour local food producers. Local events include the Trinxat festival in Puigcerdà in February and an accordion meeting at La Molina in August. It belongs to the Xanascat network of Catalan youth hostels, which mostly serves school and youth groups; reservations go through the network's central office or online.

Planoles Xanascat is a mountain hostel beside the village of Planoles, on the Rigard river in the Ribes valleys of the Ripollès, surrounded by woods, paths and mountain villages. It has 194 places and was the first hostel the Generalitat of Catalonia inaugurated as such, a mountain building for staying comfortably for a few days. Walks and hikes start from the door, peaks above 2,000 metres are within easy reach, and the ski resorts of La Molina and La Masella, the north of the Berguedà and the Cerdanya are all close, with adventure sports and fishing for those who want them. Above all, the hostel sells peace and quiet. It belongs to the Xanascat network of Catalan youth hostels and can be contacted directly or through the network's central reservations office.

Coma-ruga Xanascat is a seaside hostel at Avinguda Palfuriana 104 in Coma-ruga, part of the municipality of El Vendrell in the Baix Penedès, built long ago to host children on holiday. Xanascat sums the area up as the Mediterranean: culture, sport, thermal waters and a long beach. El Vendrell has several museums and foundations and stages cultural events, above all concerts, both in the town centre and in the seafront district. Hostel routines are posted: dinner is served from 20:30 to 21:00 and silence begins at midnight, with other times available at the hostel. It belongs to the Xanascat network of Catalan youth hostels, which mostly serves school and youth groups; reservations go through the network's central office or online.
