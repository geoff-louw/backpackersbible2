Budget Accommodation In Galicia

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

ALBERGUE BELA MUXÍA

STREET ADDRESS: Rúa Encarnación, 30, 15124 Muxía, A Coruña

PHONE: +34 687 79 82 22

WEBSITE: belamuxia.com

EMAIL: albergue@belamuxia.com

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com

Bela Muxía is a small private albergue in the fishing village of Muxía on the Costa da Morte, at the end of the Fisterra–Muxía Camino. It is about 300 metres from the village centre and a short walk from the beach. Guests have a shared kitchen and lounge, a terrace, free Wi-Fi, laundry, luggage storage, bicycle parking and a tour desk. The interior is themed around the legends, poems and history of Muxía and the Coast of Death, with a small exhibition for guests to read. On Hostelworld it scores a perfect 10 out of 10 from 42 reviews, with every category rated 10.0. Guests praise the hosts, and arriving pilgrims often mention the homemade soup and bread they are offered. Booking.com guests single out the owners and staff, the design, the kitchen, the soup and the location. Breakfast is not included, there is no curfew, and Hostelworld bookings can be cancelled free up to 48 hours before arrival.

Booking.com

Area Surf House is a surf hostel on the Muros coast of A Coruña province, roughly a 10–15-minute walk from the beaches of San Francisco and Area Maior-Louro. Beds are in shared dorms, and guests have a shared living area and a dining space with a long table. Air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, laundry service and housekeeping are provided, and there is free private parking, which helps if you are exploring this stretch of coast by car.

NÁUTICO DE RAZO

STREET ADDRESS: Rúa Paseo Praia, 14, 15107 Arnados, A Coruña

PHONE: +34 981 75 24 55

WEBSITE: nauticoderazo.com

EMAIL: info@nauticoderazo.com

FACEBOOK: facebook.com/nauticoderazo

GOOGLE RATING: 4.6 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com

Hostal Náutico de Razo sits right on Razo beach in the Carballo area of A Coruña province, with direct access to the sand; Baldaio beach is about 15–20 minutes’ walk along the coast. It is closer to a beachfront guesthouse than a dormitory hostel: there are 17 rooms with private bathrooms, a garden, a terrace, an open-air bath and a restaurant serving seafood and local dishes, with free Wi-Fi throughout and a free continental breakfast (9:00–11:00). Guests praise the beach location, the clean rooms and the hosts, and surfers mention good board storage, showers for rinsing off and a wetsuit-drying area. One guest found the restaurant closed during their stay, so check ahead if you are counting on it. There are seven beach bars close by.

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Hotel Náutico is a former hotel turned hostel, standing between the marina and the Alameda in central Vigo, a few steps from the old town (Casco Vello) and the Calle Príncipe shopping street. Rooms are either shared or double, and every room has its own private bathroom. Dorm beds have a privacy curtain, a light and a plug socket, and there is a lift, free Wi-Fi, free luggage storage, free city maps and tourist information at the desk. Breakfast is not included. Hostelworld rates it 8.4 out of 10 from 30 reviews: guests score the location (9.7) and staff (9.3) very highly, and one reviewer praised the help with planning onward travel. The weakest score is facilities (6.7). Reviewers mention slow shower drainage and the occasional cleanliness slip, and Hostelworld’s summary of guest feedback says it often feels more like a hotel than a social hostel. In-person reception is open 08:00–21:00, with check-in from 15:00.

HOSTELWORLD

Residencia Universitaria Resa O Castro is a university residence on the University of Vigo campus at Marcosende, about 7 km from the city centre, so it suits travellers with a car or those happy to take the bus. Hostelworld describes rooms with terraces and views over the surrounding hills, free wired internet, a gym and a shared kitchen. There is also a games room with table tennis and billiards, a terrace and garden, and free parking. Rooms range from singles to doubles, triples and small apartments. Check-in is 14:00 to midnight and check-out is by 12:00. Pets are not allowed.

PHONE: +34 988 06 13 22

WEBSITE: grupochamouco.com

GOOGLE RATING: 4.1 out of 5 stars

Booking.com

Albergue Augas Quentes is a small private hostel on Rúa Vicente Risco in Ourense, with three rooms. It is about a five-minute walk from the Roman Bridge, eight minutes from Ourense-Empalme train station and ten from San Lázaro Park; the cathedral is about 1.6 km away, and the Burgas thermal springs are roughly 2 km away. Guests have free Wi-Fi, laundry, a games room and a lobby with coffee and tea, and the common areas are heated and adapted for guests with reduced mobility. Reception is open limited hours, there is no on-site parking, and pets are not allowed.

PHONE: +34 604 02 43 28

WEBSITE: albergueallariz.com

EMAIL: info@albergueallariz.com

GOOGLE RATING: 4.5 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com

Albergue Salesiano is the Hostelling International youth hostel in Allariz, in the historic centre of the town on the Vía de la Plata Camino route. It has 96 places, in communal rooms with shared bathrooms and in smaller rooms with private bathrooms. Shared spaces include lounges, a games room, a large dining room and meeting rooms, and there is free Wi-Fi, outdoor courts and an amphitheatre. The building is adapted for guests with reduced mobility and has a lift. The Acearrica river beach on the River Arnoia is about 100 metres away, with a green area and a riverside path. The hostel also offers full board for groups of more than 20 people.

HOSTEL CROSS

STREET ADDRESS: Rúa da Cruz, 14, 27001 Lugo

PHONE: +34 676 83 62 11

WEBSITE: hostelcross.com

EMAIL: hostelcross@gmail.com

FACEBOOK: facebook.com/hostelcross

WHATSAPP: +34 604 02 66 05

GOOGLE RATING: 3.4 out of 5 stars

See Photos & Reviews on:

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

Hostel Cross is in the heart of Lugo’s historic centre, in the restaurant district, about two minutes’ walk from the cathedral and close to the Roman walls. You sleep in one of 48 bunk capsules, each with a lockable locker, a plug, a reading light, a curtain and a shelf, and there is a shared lounge on the third floor, a bar, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi. Breakfast costs extra (€5) and linen is not included, so check when booking. On Hostelworld it scores 8.4 out of 10 from 75 reviews, with location (9.3), value for money (9.0) and cleanliness (8.7) rated highest. Guests mention friendly staff and clean facilities, but noise is the recurring complaint: the street is lively, and one traveller reported a club below playing loud music until 4 am. Another, walking the Camino, found the bedding worn but felt the location and atmosphere made up for it. Check-in is 15:00–20:00, and automatic check-in is possible outside reception hours.
