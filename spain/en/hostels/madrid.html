Budget Accommodation In Madrid

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.
