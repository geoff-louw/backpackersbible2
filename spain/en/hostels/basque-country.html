Budget Accommodation in Basque Country

Hostels listed on Booking.com and Hostelworld

Booking.com HOSTELWORLD

A quick note about Spanish accommodation: a hostel, hostal and albergue are not necessarily the same thing. Hostels are the familiar backpacker option, while a hostal is usually a cheap private guesthouse. Albergue can mean a youth hostel, municipal hostel, mountain refuge or Camino pilgrim hostel, each with different rules. Regional languages can add to the confusion too — for example, Catalonia uses alberg and the Basque Country uses aterpetxe. There are also networks such as REAJ, Hostelling International and Catalonia's Xanascat.

A Room in the City is a hostel in the very centre of San Sebastián (Donostia), with entrances on Easo Kalea 20 and Manterola Kalea 15. It offers private and shared rooms, and the hostel says its beds were specially designed for comfort. There are social areas where guests can relax and meet other travellers, and its own site pitches the city as a mix of surfing, local food, beach time and the Old Town. Groups are described as the hostel's speciality, so it takes bookings for large parties as well as individual travellers. It is a registered tourist business (CIE 2356) and promises the best price when you book on its own website. The site is available in English, Spanish, French and Basque. The Booking.com and Hostelworld listings have photos and guest reviews.

Belle Époque is a restored guesthouse on the first floor at Alameda del Boulevard 26, in central San Sebastián. A guest on Booking.com says it is close to the sea, the city centre, shops, bars and restaurants, so you can do everything on foot, and that the host is easy to reach. Blai Blai is a small family-run hostel in Zarautz, at Nafarroa 37A, with shared rooms about three minutes on foot from the beach. The owners say everyone is welcome, from young and not-so-young travellers to groups, couples, solo guests, pilgrims and surfers, and they talk up the chance for linguistic and cultural exchange between guests. The town has a motorway exit on the A-8 and a stop on the Bilbao to San Sebastián railway, with regular buses to the surrounding coast. Bookings can be made through its own site, Booking.com or Hostelworld, and a mobile number for reservations is on the site.

Casa Rural La Palmera is a country guesthouse at Hernani Bidea 25 in Lasarte-Oria, in the green belt around San Sebastián and about ten minutes from the city. It is not a hostel: it can be rented as a whole or room by room, and each room has its own en-suite bathroom, an extra-large bed, a mini fridge and a tea and coffee station. Cats Hostel San Sebastián, formerly Koisi, is at Paseo Heriz 38, next to Ondarreta beach, and is one of four Cats hostels in Spain and Portugal. It has private and shared rooms, including female dormitories, with bunk beds and individual reading lights, plus a terrace, a bar and common areas with a kitchen and lounge. The nearest metro and tram stop is Amara, about a ten-minute walk, and the hostel says it does not use WhatsApp to communicate with guests, so contact is by phone or email.

Guests share a community kitchen, and there is a washer and dryer, luggage storage, home-automation key access and extra beds on request. No breakfast is served, but the kitchen and fridge are there for your own supplies, and there is no bar, restaurant or private parking, though reception can suggest nearby car parks. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 11:00, with reception open until 23:30 and the building accessible round the clock after online check-in. The Old Town and the Guggenheim Museum are each about a 15-minute walk away. Pets are not allowed.

Every room has air conditioning and an empty mini fridge for guests' own use, and there is free Wi-Fi throughout, daily cleaning, a lift, free luggage storage and multilingual reception staff. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00, with reception open from 14:00 to 06:00. Breakfast is not offered, a refundable 100 euro damage deposit is taken on arrival, and late check-out costs extra. The Guggenheim is about 15 minutes away on foot, the city centre is 1.1 km away and the airport is about 30 minutes by public transport. It suits couples, business travellers and anyone wanting a private room rather than a dorm.

The hostel's website shows two mobile numbers and a WhatsApp icon, and bookings are taken by phone, as it asks guests to call to reserve. This is a place for a quiet rural stay rather than a city hostel, and it suits groups who want a whole house to themselves.
