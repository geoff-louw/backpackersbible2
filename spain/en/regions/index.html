The Backpacking Regions
Of Spain

Explore the wonders of the rainbow nation. From the peaks of the Drakensberg to the otherworldly vibes of the Zulu Kingdom, find your next adventure here. The countries of Lesotho and Eswatini are included in this guide, as many backpackers visit them during their tour of South Africa.

CATALONIA

From Barcelona’s bold streets to Pyrenean peaks, medieval villages, Costa Brava coves and superb food, Catalonia has a character all its own in Spain...

EXPLORE

BARCELONA

Gaudí’s surreal architecture, Gothic streets, golden beaches, buzzing markets, tapas bars and legendary nightlife make Barcelona a feast for the senses, where Catalan culture meets Mediterranean cool...

EXPLORE

MADRID

Grand boulevards, world-class art, lively tapas bars, legendary nightlife and sun-soaked plazas make Madrid a city that never loses its energy, charm or appetite for life...

EXPLORE

ANDALUCIA

Flamenco, old white villages, Moorish palaces, beaches, tapas and mountains give Andalucía a soulful mix of culture, colour, warmth and Spanish flair...

EXPLORE

SEVILLE

Flamenco, orange-scented streets, Moorish palaces, hidden courtyards, tapas bars and sultry summer nights make Seville feel like the very soul of Spain...

EXPLORE

VALENCIA

Few cities on earth blend rich history with modern flair so effortlessly - golden beaches, futuristic architecture, historic streets and legendary paella...

EXPLORE

BASQUE COUNTRY

Wild ocean vistas meet rolling hills in a vibrant territory celebrated globally for its revolutionary gastronomy, ancient language and rich maritime traditions...

EXPLORE

GALICIA

Mist-shrouded Celtic hills, sweeping fjord-like estuaries and dramatic coastal cliffs frame a land rich in pilgrimage history, Celtic myth and exquisite seafood...

EXPLORE

THE CAMINO

Centuries of history echo along every mile as this legendary route carries travellers past sprawling vineyards, hilltop monasteries and vibrant pilgrim towns...

EXPLORE

ASTURIAS

Soaring limestone peaks and lush cider orchards drop dramatically to wild ocean beaches, creating an unspoiled paradise of cheese, coastlines and history...

EXPLORE

CANTABRIA

Prehistoric cave art, snow-capped peaks and golden surf beaches converge in a green paradise filled with charming fishing ports and rich mountain heritage...

EXPLORE

CASTILE & LEÓN

Vast high plains, towering medieval castles and ancient cathedral cities define the historic heartland where the Spanish language and royal empires were born...

EXPLORE

CASTILLA LA MANCHA

Sun-drenched plains, iconic windmills and dramatic cliffside towns define a storied landscape famously immortalized by Don Quixote, artisan cheeses and endless vineyards...

EXPLORE

TOLEDO

Perched high above the Tagus River, this ancient imperial city showcases a stunning blend of Christian, Muslim and Jewish heritage in narrow cobblestone alleys...

EXPLORE

ARAGÓN

Jagged mountain peaks, wild glacial valleys and stark desert badlands surround historic plazas, ornate palaces and a rich legacy of adventure and culture...

EXPLORE

NAVARRE

Pyrenean peaks, lush beech forests and semi-arid deserts frame a historic kingdom famous for lively bull runs, medieval castles and rich culinary traditions...

EXPLORE

LA RIOJA

Rolling vineyards, avant-garde wineries and medieval hilltop villages dot the Ebro Valley, creating Spain's most famous wine region steeped in rich history...

EXPLORE

EXTREMADURA

Vast plains and oak forests shelter magnificent Roman monuments, medieval walled towns and world-class jamón, offering an authentic, untouched slice of Spanish history...

EXPLORE

MURCIA

Sun-drenched orchards, calm coastal lagoons and dramatic arid landscapes surround a vibrant university city renowned for tapas, historic squares and year-round sunshine...

EXPLORE

BALEARIC ISLANDS

Crystal-clear waters and idyllic sandy bays meet vibrant coastal towns, where iconic Ibiza beach clubs, quiet mountain villages and UNESCO-listed heritage shine...

EXPLORE

CANARY ISLANDS

Stark lunar landscapes, lush mountain ravines and golden Atlantic beaches create a diverse island paradise famous for eternal spring weather and volcanic scenery...

EXPLORE

CEUTA & MELILLA

Sun-drenched North African outposts combine grand modernist architecture, historic seaside fortifications and a rich tapestry of cultures along the Mediterranean shore...

EXPLORE
