Spain Itineraries

Backpacking Spain Itineraries

Spain is one of the easiest countries in Europe to backpack: the trains are fast, the coaches are cheap and the hostels are good. The hard part is choosing a route. Below are our tried-and-tested itineraries, from a five-day city stay to a three-month grand tour, grouped by what matters most to you.

Every itinerary has an interactive map, daily distances, suggested hostels and a cost breakdown in euros per person, and every route is built for backpackers on a budget. If you have two weeks and want the cheapest way to see the country, start with the Shoestring Tour; if you would rather have private rooms and a few treats, try the Flashpacker's Spain.

Prices are checked each year, and fares and ticket prices change with demand, so book the big attractions (the Alhambra and the Sagrada Família especially) as early as you can.
